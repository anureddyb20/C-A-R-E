EXPECTED POSITIVE OUTCOMES ON LOCAL COMMUNITIES

This ultra-low-cost wearable monitor generates cascading positive outcomes by bridging the gap between healthcare accessibility and emergency response efficiency.

Who Will Benefit?
1. The Elderly & At-Risk Patients: Vulnerable individuals, especially those living alone or in remote areas, gain a continuous safety net without needing smartphones or expensive subscriptions.
2. Caregivers & Families: Relatives benefit from immense psychological relief, as real-time alerts eliminate the anxiety of leaving patients unmonitored.
3. Patients with Anxiety Disorders: Individuals suffering from severe panic attacks benefit from objective stress tracking (via GSR), providing a voice for their distress when they cannot verbally communicate.
4. Local Healthcare Systems: Early detection prevents minor events from escalating, drastically reducing the strain on community clinics and ambulance services.

How They Will Benefit:
- Reduced Emergency Response Times: In cardiac emergencies, seconds dictate survival. Instant local alerts enable immediate intervention, exponentially increasing survival rates.
- Democratized Access: Costing only ₹1,100, the project shatters economic barriers, ensuring life-saving technology is accessible to low-income households.
- Enhanced Dignity: Patients can safely age in place or recover at home. This automated protection helps them maintain independence, significantly improving their overall quality of life and mental well-being.
