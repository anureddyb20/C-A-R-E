1. Humanitarian Problem / Local Challenge:
Our project addresses the critical lack of affordable, continuous health monitoring for the elderly, individuals living alone, and patients in low-resource areas. Currently, continuous cardiac and stress monitoring requires expensive medical equipment or premium smartwatches that cost hundreds of dollars and mandate monthly subscriptions. Furthermore, these commercial devices rely on complex smartphone applications that are not user-friendly for the elderly. As a result, sudden cardiac events often go unnoticed until it is too late because caregivers are unaware of the emergency occurring in another room.

Beyond cardiac emergencies, this lack of accessible monitoring ignores other widespread challenges. For instance, patients suffering from severe anxiety or panic disorders often struggle to communicate distress during an episode. Additionally, in remote rural communities, the immense distance to the nearest hospital means that early detection of physiological distress is essential, yet practically non-existent due to prohibitive equipment costs.

2. Significance of the Problem:
Cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of global mortality, and in critical situations, response time dictates survival. By utilizing ultra-low-cost components (~₹1,100), our project democratizes access to life-saving technology. It provides a standalone system requiring zero technical literacy. If an abnormal heart rate or acute stress spike (via GSR) is detected, or the panic button is triggered, caregivers receive instant, localized alerts. This drastically reduces emergency response times for cardiac events, enables rapid intervention for panic attacks, and supports safe post-operative home care, ultimately providing immense psychological relief to both patients and families regardless of their economic background.
